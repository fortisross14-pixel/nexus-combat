# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Solo players of a combat-sport simulation who watch a living competitive world unfold and follow it the way a fan and team manager would. They play in sessions that mix quick simulation steps (next event, next big event, simulate a year) with long reading: rankings, fighter histories, title lineages, results. Played on desktop and phone equally; both need first-class layouts.

## Product Purpose

NEXUS: Combat Chronicle generates and simulates a fictional combat-sports universe starting in 2058: fighters age, train, win, lose, retire and enter the Hall of Fame; belts change hands; tournaments crown champions; every fight is archived. Success is a world the player wants to keep watching, where every ranking, title, nationality and record reads as meaningful history.

## Positioning

A persistent, self-running combat world rather than a single-match game: two rival brands (NOVA, ONYX), continental circuits, recurring crown events (The Surge, The Crucible, the Olympiad, The Pinnacle unifier, doubles by nation), a draft each offseason, and a permanent chronicle the player builds just by advancing time.

## Operating Context

- Up to three independent universes, each with its own seed, calendar, fighters, belts and chronicle.
- Core loop: prepare the next event, view the fight card, make picks, watch fights live or skip to results, then advance time.
- The offseason runs a draft room where the player manages brand rosters.
- Saves live in the browser (IndexedDB) with export and import to JSON.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- All simulation mechanics, game data, functionality and navigation must be preserved through any redesign.
- Navigation: Overview, Calendar, Fight Night, Tournaments, Divisions, Fighter Database, Fight Archive, Statistics, Offseason, World History; plus fighter profiles, fight reports, comparison (tale of the tape), live broadcasts and the season finale.
- React 18 + Vite + TypeScript; deployed as a static site.
- Fighter portraits are composited from layered WebP parts (src/assets/portraits); brand logos for NEXUS, NOVA and ONYX (src/assets/brands).

## Brand Commitments

- Name: NEXUS: Combat Chronicle. Brands NOVA ("Bright lights. Fast hands.") and ONYX ("Forged in the dark.") with their existing logos.
- Fighter rarity tiers (Generational, Legend, Epic, Rare and below) are part of the game's language.
- Tone: premium combat broadcast meeting a sophisticated sports-management game.

## Evidence on Hand

All content is simulated game data generated by the engine; there are no real athletes, sponsors, venues or results. Invented real-world claims (sponsors, real arenas, real broadcasters) must not be added.

## Product Principles

- The world is the product: history, rankings and titles must read as consequential.
- Information-rich without clutter: a player should scan a screen and know who matters and why.
- Every number has provenance: records, reigns and ratings link back to fights and fighters.
- Simulation speed and depth coexist: quick actions are always one step away from deep reading.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Text contrast at WCAG AA, keyboard access to every control and dialog, 44px touch targets on touch screens, and a reduced-motion path that keeps state changes legible (established in the October 2026 audit pass).
